# Code of Conduct

The SSH KeySelect community aims to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Treat others with respect and courtesy.
- Give and receive feedback constructively.
- Focus discussion on the project and its technical merits.
- Respect other people's privacy.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Repeated disruptive or abusive behavior.
- Sharing someone's private information without their consent.

This policy applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and discussions, and to public spaces when someone is representing the project.

The project maintainer may edit or remove content, reject contributions, or restrict participation when needed to keep project spaces safe and constructive. To report a concern, contact the maintainer using the contact information on [@jfut](https://github.com/jfut).
